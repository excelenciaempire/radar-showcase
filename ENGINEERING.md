# Raptor engineering

## Product identity

Supplier matching distinguishes the same product from a variant, bundle or substitute. A proposal needs attribute-level review; title similarity alone is insufficient.

## Evaluation

An operational review queue is a biased sample. The private project includes a separate matching-evaluation workflow covering positive predictions, borderline candidates and difficult rejections. This case publishes no accuracy percentage.

## Freshness

Stock observations retain timestamps. Unknown stock differs from zero stock, and a decrease does not establish a sale. Stored investigations can remain readable while a refresh runs or fails without becoming newly observed evidence.

## Economics

Cash-on-delivery calculations depend on explicit cost assumptions. Missing inputs remain unknown. Advertising-library observations support research; they do not establish another seller's profit.
